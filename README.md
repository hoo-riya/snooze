# snooze 🐑

A little app to help us actually wake up on time, kind of like Snapchat streaks but for mornings.

## How it works

- **Roll a pet:** you start by rolling a random sleepy pet: a sheep, bunny, cat, bear, frog, or the rare duck.
- **Set your wake-up goal:** pick your own time. When it's time, you pop some floating Zs on screen to prove you're actually up, not just tapping in your sleep. You have from 30 min before your goal to 15 min after.
- **Keep your streak:** wake up on time every day to grow your streak. Miss a day and it resets, unless you have a streak freeze saved up.
- **Earn coins:** you get coins for waking up on time, and more the longer your streak is. Spend them on hats, glasses, scarves and stuff for your pet, plus cute wallpapers. Some wallpapers only unlock with long streaks.
- **Squad mode:** see everyone's pets and who's awake yet. There's also a group wake-up time where everyone tries to get up together. First one up gets a badge, and whoever misses it is the Sleepyhead 😴
